How we work

How a project works

You talk directly to the person doing your work on WhatsApp. No account manager in between.

01

You tell us what you need

Send a short message on WhatsApp. Tell us what the website or app should do, and share any example you like.

02

We give a price and a date

We reply with the full price, what is included, and the delivery date. In writing. If it does not suit you, there is no charge for asking.

03

We make the first version

We build the first version and send you a test link. You can open it on your phone and check everything yourself.

04

You check it and ask for changes

Tell us what to change. Small changes in design, text and photos are part of the work. We do not charge extra for every small fix.

05

We test it properly

We check the site on phone and laptop, test all forms and buttons, and fix anything that does not work before launch.

06

We put it live

We connect your domain and put the site live. For 30 days after that, if something breaks, we fix it free.
